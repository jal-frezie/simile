The model: a simple bank account model

Next Step >>

 

We will make a model for a simple bank account, with interest paid annually at a rate of 10%, and $10 taken out every year.  The account initially contains $300.   This is what we expect to happen:

Year 1

$30 added in from interest (=0.10x300).

$10 taken out.  

Net addition is thus $20 (=$30-$10). 

Therefore the value of the account at the end of the first year is $320 (=$300+$20).

Year 2

$32 added in from interest (=0.1x320).

$10 taken out.  

Net addition is thus $22 (=$32-$10).  

Therefore the value of the account at the end of the second year is $342 (=$320+$22).

 

And so on.  Note that the increments are getting bigger each year (because the capital goes up, so the interest paid increases).  This bank account is increasing at a faster and faster rate.

 

In order to make this model in Simile, we need a model diagram with one compartment (to represent the amount of money held in the bank account), and two flows (to represent the gain of money from interest and the loss of money by withdrawal.   One flow (interest) will go into the compartment, while the other flow (withdrawal) will come out of the compartment.   We will also use a variable to represent the interest rate (10%, or 0.1).
